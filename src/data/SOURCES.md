# Massachusetts municipalities

Source: US Census Bureau CitySDK, 2022 cartographic county subdivisions (Massachusetts, FIPS 25).
https://github.com/uscensusbureau/citysdk/blob/master/v2/GeoJSON/500k/2022/25/county-subdivision.json

Retrieved 2026-10-11. Contains exactly 351 unique municipality names and GEOIDs, across 14 counties. Only municipality name, slug, county, city/town type and census GEOID are retained; geometry is not used. US federal government geographic data is public domain. This list provides geographical names, not business availability, population, or license rights.
